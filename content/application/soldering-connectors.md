When soldering D-SUB connectors it is too easy to make mistakes, so I created this webapp to display the pins, highlight the ones being used, and allow the drawing to be rotated to match the orientation of the part being worked on.

![](/img/connectors_dsub15.png)

<!--more-->

This app is hosted free at [https://kgolding.github.io/connectors](https://kgolding.github.io/connectors/?pins=1+2+3+8+10+11+12+15)
